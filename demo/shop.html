How this works

Three products and one script tag each. Click Pay, pick the coin you hold on any chain, and the shop receives exactly the price in USDC on NEAR. You can see the quote and the deposit address without sending anything.

Real money. These buttons pay tome.near, the account that built this demo. A payment you send is a real payment, and nothing ships: it is a demo.

Local mode. The buttons open the local pay page () with a mocked backend. Nothing here is real: no quote, no address and no payment.

The tag on each product:

  
<script src="https://nearswaps.com/checkout.js"
        data-to="tome.near" data-amount="2" data-asset="usdc"
        data-label="Demo shop: coffee" data-theme="light"></script>


  

    
nearswaps checkout: the buyer pays from any wallet on any chain, NEAR Intents settles it, and the shop receives the invoiced amount. nearswaps' fee is included in the quote. Accept payments on your own site.
